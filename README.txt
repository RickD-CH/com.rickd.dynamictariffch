Brings the 15-minute prices of a Swiss dynamic electricity tariff into Homey, using the Innostrom API from Swisspower. Works with AEW Classic Dynamic (from January 2027) and any other Swiss utility on the same Innostrom platform.

Three dashboard widgets show today's and tomorrow's prices, the cheapest block and your cost against your old tariff. Flow cards shift devices to the cheapest hours. If you want more, switch on the built-in energy management: it charges your EV from solar surplus or in the cheapest quarter-hours before departure, controls your home battery and devices, protects your main fuse and shows in the outlook what happens next. Everything is only simulated until you switch it live.
