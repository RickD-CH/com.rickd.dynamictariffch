Brings the 15-minute prices of a Swiss dynamic electricity tariff into Homey, using the Innostrom API from Swisspower. Works with AEW Classic Dynamic (from January 2027) and any other Swiss utility built on the same Innostrom platform.

See today's and tomorrow's prices at a glance in three dashboard widgets: the price curve with the cheapest block, an hourly price list, and your accumulated cost against your old tariff. Flow cards shift EV chargers, boilers and heat pumps to the cheapest hours, and an optional cost comparison with a battery analysis shows what the dynamic tariff really brings you.
