Brings the 15-minute prices of a Swiss dynamic electricity tariff into Homey, using the Innostrom API from Swisspower. Works with AEW Classic Dynamic (from January 2027) and any other Swiss utility built on the same Innostrom platform.

Pair a metering point with its API token, and this app fetches today's and tomorrow's quarter-hourly prices automatically and keeps them current. See the price at a glance, then let your flows act on it.

THREE WIDGETS FOR YOUR DASHBOARD
- Innostrom price: the current price with its level (cheap, normal, expensive), rank of the day, the day's low, average and high, the cheapest 2-hour block, and the price curve for today and tomorrow with your reference tariff as a dashed line. One tap shows your grid import on top of the curve, so you can see whether you bought power at good or bad moments.
- Innostrom price list: all 24 hours of today or tomorrow in a compact grid, color-coded by price level, with the cheapest and most expensive 2-hour blocks marked. Tap an hour to see its four quarter-hour prices.
- Innostrom costs: your accumulated cost with the dynamic tariff against your reference tariff, so you can see what the switch actually brings.

FLOW CARDS
Shift consumers - EV chargers, boilers, heat pumps, washing machines - to the cheapest hours. React to price changes and thresholds, find the cheapest block of hours in a time window, from now on, or within the next hours, check whether the current price is among the day's cheapest or most expensive, or get the hourly prices of today and tomorrow as JSON, for example for an energy management app.

COST COMPARISON WITHOUT EXTRA PERMISSIONS
Report meter readings from your flows (grid import and export, battery, and single consumers such as an EV or a heat pump) and the app calculates costs, savings and the battery benefit. Enter your old tariff directly, or calculate it from the lines of your bill, with validity periods for tariff changes. A shadow calculation shows what an optimal battery charging plan would have brought within your battery's charge range, without controlling anything.

APP SETTINGS
An overview with live values and the price curve or table, a costs tab with curves for each of the last 14 days, setup help, and tools to manage the collected data. Available in English and German, with light and dark appearance.
